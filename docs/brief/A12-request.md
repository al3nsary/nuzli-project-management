## Decisions taken «كما تقترح»
- **Project participants:** added to projects by admins.
- **Intermediate rights:** all three levels are built; supervisors default to Contributor.
- **Card printing:** admins only.
- **Workers:** adding or removing one needs admin approval; editing their data does not.
- **Dual role:** allowed, with a warning and a badge.
- **Project cover image:** optional, with a generated default.
- **Colour:** stays the approved A11 violet `#5030E5`.
